## Verb progression

`dictionary form → ます → ません → ました → ませんでした`

来る and する are handled explicitly: 来ます・来ません・来ました・来ませんでした and します・しません・しました・しませんでした. 帰る is treated as a Group 1 / う-verb exception despite ending in る.

## Adjective progression

`basic → negative → past → past negative`

The four systems are represented by canonical records. いい is not introduced, so its irregular conjugation is not required.

## Kanji milestone

Day 39 deliberately focuses recognition on 日, 月, 水, and 学, using 日本, 月曜日, 水, and 学生. Day 40 reinforces 火, 木, 金, 土, 人, and 本. Each is linked to an existing kanji record and receives recognition practice; handwriting and exhaustive readings are deferred.
