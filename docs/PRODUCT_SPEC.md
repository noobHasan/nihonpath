# Product Spec

Nihon Path takes a true zero-Japanese beginner through a structured foundation in kana, reading, writing, listening, speaking, grammar, practical Japan, and JLPT N5 readiness. It is designed to continue into N4 after the 90-day foundation.

The product must develop learner production and understanding, not merely vocabulary cards, grammar definitions, multiple-choice quizzes, or translated examples. It is not a fluency promise and does not guarantee independent life in Japan.
