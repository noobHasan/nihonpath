## Grammar progression

Day 61 explains the purpose of て-form. Day 62 teaches Group 1 formation, including う・つ・る → って, む・ぶ・ぬ → んで, く → いて, ぐ → いで, and す → して. Day 63 teaches Group 2 and irregular formation. Days 64–67 consolidate prior Phase 4 patterns. Days 68–69 introduce the only new formal grammar in this phase. Day 70 consolidates Phase 3B preferences.

## Japanese checks

- 行く → 行って, never 行いて.
- 帰る is Group 1: 帰る → 帰って.
- 来る → 来て（きて）.
- `～たいです` is primarily framed as the speaker’s desire; ～たがる is deferred.
- `～ませんか` is distinguished from plain `～ません` through context and the invitation function.
- 好き and 嫌い remain な-adjective-type predicates using が.
- `あまり好きではありません` is supported by the new `vocab-amari` record.
- No `V dictionary + のが好きです` nominalization is introduced.
